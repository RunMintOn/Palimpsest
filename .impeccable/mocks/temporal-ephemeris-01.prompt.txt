PRODUCT CONTEXT
Palimpsest is a desktop Obsidian plugin. While someone writes, it automatically finds semantically related passages from their older notes and shows those source passages in the Obsidian sidebar. The user does not need to stop and search. It is for people who have accumulated years of journals, reflections, excerpts, and writing material that they rarely see again. Its central value is reconnecting people with their past thinking, while also helping them reuse knowledge. Indexing and retrieval run locally with Ollama. It is currently a beta installed through BRAT.
