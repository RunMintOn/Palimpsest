No separate search.

Palimpsest stays beside your writing in Obsidian.

  1. 01

    Index locally

    Your Markdown notes are split into passages and embedded through your local Ollama service.

  2. 02

    Keep writing

    After you pause, the current note becomes a semantic query. No keywords and no context switch.

  3. 03

    Rediscover

    Related source passages appear in the sidebar, ready to open, link, or quote.

Your archive stays
on your machine.

Embedding and retrieval run through Ollama. The vector index lives in local IndexedDB and is isolated by Vault.
